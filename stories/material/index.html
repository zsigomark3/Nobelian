About Material

Material

Quality and craft at the heart of every piece.

We source natural and premium materials for our bags, scarves, gloves, and charms. Each is chosen for durability, comfort, and timeless appeal.

Leather

Full-grain and vegetable-tanned leather from trusted tanneries. Develops a rich patina over time and stands up to daily use.

Used in: Bags, straps, charms

Silk

Mulberry silk for scarves and linings. Light, breathable, and soft against the skin, with a subtle sheen.

Used in: Scarves, linings, accessories

Wool

Fine merino and blended wools for warmth and structure. Naturally temperature-regulating and long-lasting.

Used in: Scarves, gloves, cold-weather pieces

Linen

European linen for a crisp, breathable hand. Ideal for scarves and lightweight accessories in warmer seasons.

Used in: Scarves, seasonal accessories

Brass & hardware

Solid brass fittings and closures. Antique or polished finishes that age gracefully and stay secure.

Used in: Bags, charms, closures
