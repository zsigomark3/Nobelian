Returns & Exchanges

Our return policy and how to initiate a return.

Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with your order, you may return it within 14 days of delivery in accordance with EU consumer rights.

Conditions

  • Items must be unused, in their original condition, and in original packaging.
  • Personalised or custom-made items cannot be returned unless defective.
  • Sale items are eligible for return under the same conditions.

How to Return

  1. Contact us via our contact form with your order number and reason for return.
  2. We will provide you with a return shipping label and instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely and ship it back to us.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will process your refund within 5–10 business days. The refund will be issued to your original payment method.

Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item, please initiate a return and place a new order for the desired item.

Defective Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item, please contact us immediately. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost.
