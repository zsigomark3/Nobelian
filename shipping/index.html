Shipping

Delivery information and shipping options.

Delivery Areas

We currently ship within the European Union. International shipping options are being expanded — please contact us for availability outside the EU.

Shipping Times

  • Standard delivery (EU): 5–8 business days
  • Express delivery (EU): 2–4 business days

Please note that handcrafted items may require additional preparation time of 1–3 business days before dispatch.

Shipping Costs

  • Standard delivery: €9.90
  • Express delivery: €19.90
  • Free shipping: on orders over €300

Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number so you can follow your package.

Questions?

For any shipping-related questions, please use our contact form.
