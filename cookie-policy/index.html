What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and understand how you interact with the site.

How we use cookies

We categorise cookies into three groups:

1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function. They do not require your consent.

NamePurposeDurationType
cc_cookie Stores your cookie consent preferences 6 months First-party

2. Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website. They are only set after you give consent.

NamePurposeDurationProvider
_ga Distinguishes unique users (Google Analytics) 2 years Google
_ga_* Maintains session state (Google Analytics 4) 2 years Google
_gid Distinguishes users for 24 hours 24 hours Google
_gat Throttles request rate 1 minute Google

3. Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used for advertising purposes. They are only set after you give consent.

NamePurposeDurationProvider
_fbp Facebook tracking pixel 3 months Meta (Facebook)
_fbc Facebook click identifier 3 months Meta (Facebook)

Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage for the following purposes:

KeyPurposeCategory
nobelian-preferred-language Remembers your language preference Necessary (functionality)
nobelian-auth-token Authentication session token (only when logged in) Necessary (authentication)
nobelian-user Basic user profile for UI display (only when logged in) Necessary (authentication)

Legal Basis

Necessary cookies and local storage: Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — required for the website to function.
Analytics and marketing cookies: Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — only set after explicit user consent.
